From enquiry to visit

  1. Website enquiries save automatically in the private Register and email Alice and Amanda. There is no need to copy the enquiry across.
  2. A parent opens Copy of Alice’s Pet Pals – Private Register in Google Sheets using an account with access. In Bookings, review the enquiry, confirm suitability and record the agreed date, time and fee.
  3. Add any care instructions or emergency details agreed at the meet-and-greet directly to the private Register. After the visit, mark it complete and record payment.
